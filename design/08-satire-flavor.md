# The Flavor Bible

> Voice, eras, content banks, the conspiracy layer, media canonization, and the speedrun framing. The research files carry the full corpora with sources (`research/gaming-enshittification.md`, `societal-satire.md`, `billionaires-decay.md`, `conspiracy-media.md`); this doc sets the rules for using them and indexes the load-bearing pieces.

## 1. Voice rules

1. **Deadpan corporate.** The game speaks in the language of the systems it mocks — press releases, compliance disclosures, investor updates. No winking. (The Onion formula: institutional voice + one precise fake statistic + one load-bearing noun that inverts the premise. The headline does 100% of the work.)
2. **Curtain always pulled.** Every parodied dark pattern states what it is, in the tooltip, at maximum disclosure. The joke is the sincerity, never a trick on the player.
3. **Numbers are real.** When a ticker cites a statistic, it's a sourced one (the research files are the citation trail; a "confidence: verify" list travels with each). The satire is juxtaposition, not invention.
4. **One card sits silent.** Some lore cards carry no joke (deaths of despair; the GI Bill's locked door). The melancholy layer works because it's occasionally allowed to just be true.
5. **Never punch at players, employees-as-people, or victims.** Targets: systems, incentives, executives-as-archetypes, ourselves.
6. **Dark-content calibration:** oligarch-impunity satire (the island, the bunker, the guards) stays at the systemic-absurdity level — sealed court records as a ticker line, never lurid specifics. If a joke needs a real victim to land, cut it.

## 4. The conspiracy layer

System mechanics live in `09-events.md §3` (Conspiracy Pressure meter). The flavor arc:

1. **Early:** you're beneath notice. One Time-Cube-style outsider theorist NPC has a page about your garage (seven colors, 40,000 words).
2. **Growth:** theories spawn scaled to tier — 5G-tier (mast arson events, "these towers were not ours and also were not towers"), microchip-tier, lizard-tier ("4% of registered voters… within tolerance"). The **QAnon-as-ARG machinery** is simulated honestly: drops/crumbs/bakers, self-sealing belief ("The Storm (Delayed): believer confidence +40%"), guided apophenia ("your earnings PDF contains 322 pages. The forums have noticed."). Published rule: **suppression, debunking, and silence all raise belief. The two working responses: monetize it, or make it true.**
3. **Monetize:** the Denver path — `The Gargoyle Pivot` (+$8M, "welcome to Illuminati headquarters"), `Merch Vertical: Supplements` (165:1), conspiracy-tour tickets.
4. **The lanyard midgame:** invitations to the *real* secret societies arrive at Clout thresholds — and they're **disappointing networking events**: Davos badge tiers (you may be Ice-Village-only), Bilderberg's Chatham House Rule upgrade ("attributable quotes −100%"), Bohemian Grove ("Rule 1 is no business talk. You close three acquisitions before lunch." — and the lore card that the one confirmed world-altering decision there was the Manhattan Project), Sun Valley fleece vest (cosmetic), Skull & Bones (exactly 15 recruits/year — a resource cap).
5. **The inversion (Tier 7):** the theories become true. Dead-internet: your own bots ARE the majority of your users. The barge WAS hiding something. And the ancient lore — Anunnaki engineering a labor force to mine gold — resolves as **previous transcended players**, whose worlds were workloads, as yours is (rack 42).
6. **Counter-conspiracy:** the player can fund a Birds-Aren't-Real-style parody movement — a pressure-release valve that converts Conspiracy Pressure into Clout ("a safe space to laugh at it") — the game's own thesis, purchasable in-game.
