## House conventions for research dossiers (added 2026-07-27 after the corpus audit)

The audit found the corpus **unusually accurate but unusually untraceable**: every headline number that could be reached checked out exactly, yet **10 of 25 dossiers carry zero source URLs while their headers claim to have fetched sources.** Three errors reached design docs before anyone noticed. These conventions are binding on new dossiers and should be applied retroactively when a file is next touched.

**1. Two orthogonal axes, never one glyph.** The audit found the glyphs collide across files — `✅/⚠️` means *epistemic confidence* in four dossiers, `🟢/🟡/🔴` means *legal risk* in another, and `🟢` means *"good material"* in three more. **A copy-drafting agent reading 🟢 as "verified" will ship unverified numbers.** From now on:

| Axis | Glyphs | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Provenance** | `[V]` verified against a fetched URL · `[P]` plausible/secondary · `[M]` model knowledge, unverified | Borrowed from `pacing-science.md`, which the audit named the corpus's best-disciplined file |
| **Legal risk** | 🟢 safe · 🟡 fictionalize · 🔴 never | Only ever legal. Never epistemic. |

**2. Every factual claim carries a URL or an explicit `[M]`.** A dossier header that claims sources were fetched while the body contains none is the single defect that made this audit necessary.

**3. Every dossier carries a legal safe/fictionalize matrix.** Only 8 of 25 do. The complete standard already exists across `internet-culture.md §6` (three-tier + archetype table + drafting rules), `culture-genalpha.md §7` (the *"who is the subject of the verb?"* test), `culture-boomer.md`'s MTV→CLIPWIRE worked example, and `compliance.md §6` — **promote those, don't reinvent.**

**4. Excluded content is absolute and applies to research files, not just shipped copy.** No named real minors; no *depiction of, reference to, or content derived from* individual suicide, self-harm or child death; no named living private individuals in a mocking frame — archetypes and composites instead. **One deliberate carve-out (owner decision 2026-07-28): sourced population-level statistics may appear as silent lore cards with no joke attached** — `design/08` voice rule 4's deaths-of-despair card stands. The line is *individuals and depiction* vs *a sourced number allowed to just be true*. The audit found breaches *inside files that stated the rule themselves*, which is how you know the rule needs to live here rather than per-file.

**5. Contradicting a design doc is a feature.** `creature-battler.md` overturned `design/04 §2` with simulation and it was the most valuable output of the day. When research contradicts design, **flag it as `DESIGN-GAP:` in the design doc and escalate — never silently resolve it either way.**
