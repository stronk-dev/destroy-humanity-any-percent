# Balance Epoch 2 — Faction Foundation

Registers the four Phase-0 faction identities and their interdependence-stock cycle. Incorporation
does not change production rates in this epoch: every faction modifier list is empty. Open Source
incorporation binds the existing Commons Compact at a 130,000 ppm tithe; the other factions leave
Compact membership to the ordinary sign/decline flow.

The epoch also introduces attended-time stock accrual at one unit per minute, capped visibly at
100,000 units. Existing runs remain playable under their pinned Epoch-1 constants; new runs pin
the Epoch-2 artifact bundle.
