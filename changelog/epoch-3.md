# Balance Epoch 3 — Guild Foundation

The Guild catalog joins the immutable constants identity. It declares the automatic two-percent
tithe, deterministic half-stock member exchange, quarter-stock NPC fallback, per-boundary intake
cap, and the structural membership limits used by the Guild Model.

The Phase-0 stock-consumption bonus remains exactly zero: exchange state and receipts are live,
but no production advantage is silently attached to consumed units in this epoch.

Cap migration: no existing hardcap is lowered. The 120-unit intake limit is new per-boundary
exchange capacity, not a reduction of persisted faction stock.
