# Atomic capability outcome audit

Coordinate: product tree `190a4fa`; 2026-08-21. This audit attaches independent evidence verdicts
to the complete structural population in `capability-atomization-inventory.tsv`. The machine-checked
result is `capability-outcome-ledger.tsv`; `capability-outcome-validator.mjs` rejects denominator,
identity, shape, vocabulary, vacuous-promotion, and authority-route failures.

## Result

The 121 section-level design rows resolve to **433 independently falsifiable outcomes**. The first
structural pass reported 432, but evidence attachment exposed one invalid compound: `P-004.02`
combined exact cosmetic odds with the mounted dark-pattern disclosure. They now have different
rows and verdicts. This correction is append-recorded rather than hidden.

The three integration rows are not three independent gameplay systems. They are three design views
of the same narrow bootstrap path: server-anonymous account creation, starting from the Vision
Slide, and receiving that default identity. The ledger therefore does **not** establish an
integrated first hour, Tier 1, next run, account-recovery path, minigame, MMO loop, or release
platform.

## Material corrections produced by atomization

- “Server-authoritative and published formulas” is no longer one green-ish row: current production
  authority is a bounded primitive; public formula consumption is absent.
- Tier-1's reply-all multiplier is not automation. The automation outcome is absent even though
  the manual action can be multiplied.
- Prestige/Run End is not one partial feature. Early offers and several result fields are partial;
  carry state and route consequence are backend-only; player continuation is absent.
- Five category records do not let the player choose a speedrun category. The projector hardcodes
  `any_percent`, so category choice is backend/data-only.
- Static cosmetic disclosure does not imply lootbox odds, acquisition, or equipment. Disclosure is
  partial; the other three outcomes are absent.
- Responsive markup does not imply accessibility. The 320 px outcome is absent on a direct
  three-engine failure; supported zoom and keyboard/assistive completion remain fixture-only.
- Historical T0–T1 pacing runs do not prove the current coordinate. The first-generator and Tier-1
  targets are blocked on a valid current run; later pacing outcomes are absent.
- Declarative loaders for economy, routes, achievements, factions, guilds, and copy are bounded
  content-author primitives. Events are absent, and minigame extension remains backend-only.

## Evidence controls and limits

The final validator passes 433 ordered identities and fires all four seeded failures:
`dropped-outcome`, `duplicate-outcome`, `vacuous-promotion`, and `missing-route`. Every promoted row
names its producer, consumer, current data, and executable witness. Every other row retains the
authority route inherited from the exact design parent or a narrower RP/research route named in its
evidence limit.

This is a repository-evidence audit, not participant research and not a release ruling. Static
absence findings are coordinate-bound; they do not turn every future design promise into 1.0 scope.
The ledger deliberately gives no umbrella credit for an archived RFC, package, generated type,
fixture component, or parent summary. Product changes remain gated by accepted RFCs and the
cross-party review protocol.
