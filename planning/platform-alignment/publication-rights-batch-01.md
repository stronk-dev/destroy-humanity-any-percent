## Method

Each file was read completely, then checked for:

- workstation paths, email addresses, credential/key forms, private hosts and sibling-repository
  implementation details;
- long or unattributed quotations;
- named-person allegations or sensitive personal data;
- source links and explicit separation of verified, partial, model-derived and project-synthesis
  claims;
- wording that could be mistaken for current implementation authority;
- a historical/as-of label for claims that can age.

The review does not re-prove every external technical statement. Time-sensitive facts retain their
dated research status and must be re-verified before they become a current dependency, release
claim, or player-facing statistic.
