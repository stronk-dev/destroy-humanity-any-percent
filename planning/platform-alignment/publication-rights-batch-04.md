### `design/research/compliance.md` — PUBLIC SYNTHESIS + CURRENT LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED

The file properly says it is not legal advice, but its body repeatedly states legal outcomes as
settled: lawful bases, child-consent consequences, DSA classification/exemptions, EAA scope,
COPPA exposure, takedown duties, licensing strategy and the absence of several obligations. It also
contains explicit unverified items and time-sensitive 2026 litigation/regulatory status.

A public artifact should be a short issue-and-evidence register, not operational legal advice. It
should:

- separate facts about the currently implemented Phase-0 preview from hypothetical feed, guild,
  telemetry, minors, provider and monetization scenarios;
- link each legal claim directly to the authoritative provision/guidance and date it;
- describe unresolved interpretations and owner choices without declaring exemptions final;
- route retention, export, deletion, moderation, age and hosting requirements through D-008,
  D-009, D-015, D-017 and accepted RFCs;
- require current qualified legal review before privacy policy, ToS or public launch.

The raw dossier needs a named private-source store or explicit verified cleanup after its public
synthesis is accepted.
