three predeclared historical slug mappings. `archived-rfc-risk-inventory.tsv` records every row,
including literal line/open-box/provenance-token counts, active-RFC links, canonical docs, existing
RP routes, score, selection reason, and verdict.

The deep sample contains 20 archives. It includes every open plan, every score at least nine, every
sampled archive owning a current CI/dependency/release-relevant RP route, all ten required domains,
and four low-risk controls (`doctrine`, `meters`, `purchasable`, plus the bounded numeric case).
The remaining 26 rows are structural-only and receive no semantic approval from this pass.

The score behaved as intended: old records without current-style `Reviewed range:` tokens were
prioritized but were not retroactively invalidated merely for their historical format. The three
active direct archive links are Balance Harness, Commons Compact, and Geometric Affordability.

## Commands and validity

- `make test-go GO_PACKAGES='./decimal ./economy ./production ./save ./fiscal ./routes
  ./routeprojection ./guild ./gameserver ./pitch ./harness' GO_TEST_FLAGS='-count=1'` passed. The
  selected package run took the harness package through 27.855 s. Database tests skipped outside
  their declared URL and therefore receive no integration credit from this command.
- `make copy-check` passed at 208 keys and emitted 161 orphan warnings. That green is valid for
  grammar/generation/history, but the warning classifier's inability to recognize live `t()` calls
  is the finding; a warning-only report cannot be promoted to a dead-copy oracle.
- `make test-save-integration` passed through the declared Docker/Postgres service with
  `-run Integration ./... -count=1`. Account, Commons projection, gameserver, Game UI, Guild,
  leaderboard, minigame, production, replay verification, Route projection, and Save integration
  packages executed; packages reporting `[no tests to run]` receive no integration credit.
- A focused client Fiscal invocation emitted no denominator for more than 60 seconds and was
  terminated. It is an **invalid run**, not a pass or a budget signal. Current cross-runtime Fiscal
  evidence therefore comes from inspected shared fixtures/prior bounded lifecycle execution, while
  the fresh cold result above is Go-only.

certificate. Most sampled rows remain proven or honestly qualified within their literal scope;
every current defect, regression, and record gap is named above rather than collapsed into one
pass/fail count. The smallest authorized consequence is record/ruling-
