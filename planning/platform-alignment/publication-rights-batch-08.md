game-specific mechanics are `[M]`. That honesty makes it useful private reasoning, but the body
then calls itself a TL;DR for an implementer, declares a complete terminal grammar, assigns
mechanics across T7/T8, proposes exact `min()`/allocation/attrition structures, repeats exact copy
and presents a legal-safe matrix. Its references resolve mostly to other internal notebooks and
model knowledge rather than direct primary sources. Several recommendations would be new design or
RFC choices, not consequences of the cited games.

The public synthesis should separate three things: verified genre patterns, existing Cloud Clicker
constraints, and unresolved design questions. Reverify any comparative mechanic through primary
or authoritative sources; retain only the bounded conclusion that closed-form allocation is
compatible with the server/lazy-math laws. Extractor placement, value drift, T7/T8 ownership,
formulas, copy and ending sequencing stay owner/design/RFC work. Replace categorical IP statements

### `design/research/soul-mechanic.md`

This file usefully declares the bounded-meter versus spendable-currency owner question and marks
many unverified details. It nevertheless embeds numerous quotations, named commercial-game
mechanics, exact illustrative thresholds, Soul debits/recovery behavior, player copy and a strong
hybrid recommendation. It also makes categorical copyright/trademark safety claims and prescribes
how the game must handle self-harm/suicide-adjacent interpretation. Those are legal, safety,
editorial and owner decisions; research cannot adopt them by confidence label.

Publish a short synthesis of the evidence-backed design tension: imposed meters can become chores,
explicit spends can commodify the sincere resource, and symptoms may communicate better than a
wallet counter. Bind every retained quotation and factual mechanic to a direct source and omit
unnecessary protected expression. Exact Soul movement, recovery, pet feedback, thresholds, copy
and ending consequences remain with the owner and accepted successor RFC. Legal and self-harm
language requires current qualified/editorial review. The raw notebook needs a specifically named
private store or verified cleanup.
