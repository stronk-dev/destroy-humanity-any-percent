## File findings

### `design/research/culture-millennial.md`

The file itself says the original dossier was lost to context compaction, the surviving artifact is
partial, its source/legal matrix is missing and the pass should be rerun before drafting copy. Its
body nevertheless includes unsourced debt, wealth, labor, company and platform claims plus exact
ticker/event/lore lines. Honor its own limitation: rerun with claim-level sources, then publish a
short cohort synthesis that distinguishes observed history from a generational stereotype and
routes every proposed line through the copy authority.

### `design/research/culture-pre-boomer.md`

This notebook covers valuable historical material but accumulates short quotations and precise
claims about IBM, Bell Labs, pensions, segregation, Holocaust-related conduct, named historical
figures, media scandals and government policy, with many `[M]`/warning items and a final source list
rather than claim-level durable links. It then turns those facts into exact mechanics, irreversible
choices, ticker text and ending copy.

The public synthesis must be materially shorter, primarily sourced, careful about contested
history, and exclude Holocaust/racial injustice as game texture. Historical harms may inform the
voice but require a dedicated editorial/owner ruling before any player-facing treatment. The raw
notebook needs a named private store or explicit verified cleanup.

### `design/research/culture-genx.md`

This is a broad creative sourcebook rather than a publication-ready research record. It compiles
many brands, films, shows, characters, taglines, dialogue fragments, financial figures and living-
person material, much of it through a single Wikipedia source list. It also provides a full prop
bible, named analogues and dozens of near-final mechanics/copy lines. Its own caveats leave several
work, technology and cohort claims uncited.

The public synthesis should retain only sourced era patterns needed by the voice/design work,
avoid reproducing protected expression, label cohort generalizations as framing rather than fact,
and keep proposed copy separate until owner adoption. The raw notebook requires a named private
store or explicit cleanup.

### `design/research/culture-genalpha.md`

This raw dossier is not public-eligible. It intentionally handles the highest-risk population in
the corpus and still identifies a child creator/channel, documents allegations and enforcement
involving abuse/grooming/CSAM networks, names living creators and current companies, summarizes
active lawsuits/regulatory actions, cites third-party take-rate allegations, includes child-health
and climate claims, and contains extensive exact player/endgame copy. Several primary sources were
blocked or absent and many 2025–26 facts are highly time-sensitive.

The public artifact should be an editorially reviewed synthesis centered on durable principles:

- children and parents are never the satirical target;
- commercialization, platform incentives and institutional decisions are the target;
- no real child, child death, self-harm circumstance or recognizable child-derived scenario enters
  game content;
- allegations and current enforcement require primary-source attribution and legal/editorial
  review;
- health, development, education, climate and legal claims require primary expert sources;
- exact copy remains owner-authored and separately adopted.

The raw notebook requires a specifically named restricted source store or explicit verified
cleanup; a generic local ignore is not a durable privacy/sensitivity contract.
