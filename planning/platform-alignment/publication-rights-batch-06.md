## File findings

### `design/research/culture-boomer.md`

The dossier contains useful system-level historical themes—counterculture becoming capital,
retirement risk transfer, research-lab commercialization, media/distribution power and asset
inequality—but it is a very large creative/source compilation. It names living people and firms,
summarizes criminal/civil matters and allegations, reproduces many short quotations and commercial
media references, makes legal-safety judgments, and provides exact mechanics and player copy. Most
claims route only to a final Wikipedia topic list; several quotations and wealth claims are
explicitly unverified.

The public synthesis should keep a small number of primarily sourced system mechanisms, include
the cohort's internal inequality/Generation-Jones counterargument, remove generational blame,
avoid protected expression, and route every proposed mechanic/line through owner/design authority.
The raw notebook needs a named private store or explicit verified cleanup.

### `design/research/culture-genz.md`

This dossier does make a strong editorial rule—target systems, never the young person—but it is not
safe to publish unchanged. It combines current platform acquisitions/metrics, third-party
accusations about child developers, active political/labor/economic facts, mental-health and
climate findings, named living people (including likely minors), protected shows/films/music,
verbatim frameworks and a large slang/copy corpus. Numerous facts are marked unverified or
time-sensitive and much of the evidence is routed through Wikipedia/Know Your Meme rather than
primary sources.

The public synthesis should preserve the systems-not-youth voice rule and only a bounded set of
source-linked themes: platform sunset, valueless retention numbers, authenticity commercialization,
work-to-rule reframing, entry-level labor pressure and known-versus-used trust. Health, climate,
labor and cohort claims need primary/expert sources. Living private individuals/minors and copied
media expression stay out. Exact strings remain owner-copy candidates, not research output.
