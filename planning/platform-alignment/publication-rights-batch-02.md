## Why these are blocking, not editorial polish

- A public research file that recommends a rejected numeric library or hidden win-rate controller
  becomes a second architecture authority even if every current RFC is correct.
- Exact proposed player copy in a research notebook can be mistaken for adopted content and bypass
  the owner-author rule.
- A list of links does not cure cumulative verbatim copying or unattributed archive excerpts.
- Publishing a dossier that itself says named allegations are internal-only defeats its own risk
  analysis.
- An end-of-file correction does not make a contradictory 900-line body safe for a reader who
  stops earlier or quotes a stale section.
